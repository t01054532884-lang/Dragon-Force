Dragon Force Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 · Replaces the previous policy (effective November 18, 2026). 한국어

SquadCode (representative: Junsoo Oh; "we", "the operator") protects the personal information of people who play the mobile game "Dragon Force" ("the Game") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. The Game does not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

1. Information we process and why

InformationPurposeHow it is collected

Sign in with Apple identifier (a unique user value provided by Apple)Linking your Apple account so you can continue after changing devices or reinstallingWhen you tap "Link with Apple". We do not request your name or email

Purchase records (product ID, store transaction ID, purchase time, items granted, refund status) and Skin Roulette records (keys owned, draw results and times)Delivering paid items, drawing the Skin Roulette on our server to prevent tampering, checking duplicate delivery and purchase limits, restoring purchases, handling refunds, keeping transaction records required by lawThrough the payment processor when you pay on the App Store

Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple; we never receive them.

2. Retention and deletion

  • Account identifier, Sign in with Apple identifier, and game progress: kept until you delete your account. You can delete it immediately in the Game under "Settings → Delete Account", which erases your progress on our server and on the device. For accounts linked to Apple, we also revoke the Sign in with Apple token.
  • Purchase records: under the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, records of payment and delivery are kept for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints or disputes for 3 years. Even after account deletion, these are stored separately from other information for that period and then destroyed.
  • When the retention period ends, information is destroyed without delay in a way that cannot be recovered.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal information with third parties except where required by law. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites (App Tracking Transparency).

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time. Account deletion is available directly in the Game under "Settings → Delete Account". For other requests, contact us at the address below and we will respond without delay.

6. Children

The Game does not collect information that identifies children, such as names or contact details. Purchases by users under 14 require the consent of a parent or legal guardian, and Apple's parental approval features such as "Ask to Buy" can be used.

7. Security

Server records can be read and written only by the account's owner, and purchase records are isolated so that only our server can access them. All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
