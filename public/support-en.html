FAQ

Where is my progress saved?
On your device and on our server (cloud). If you tap "Link with Apple" in Settings, you can continue after changing devices or reinstalling. A guest account that is not linked cannot be recovered after the app is deleted.

How do I delete my account?
In the game, go to "Settings → Delete Account". All progress and currencies are erased and this cannot be undone.

I didn't receive an item I bought.
Restart the game or tap "Restore Purchases" in the shop. If that doesn't help, contact us above with the purchase time and item name. Refunds are requested through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Where can I see chest odds?
Open "Odds · Guarantees" in the shop to see odds by grade and item, and the guarantee rules.

Can I switch languages?
The game follows your device language at first. You can switch anytime in "Settings → Language".
